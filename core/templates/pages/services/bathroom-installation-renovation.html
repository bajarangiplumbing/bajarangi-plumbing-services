  • Inspect and agree the plumbing scope

    I check existing supply, waste, stack positions and access, then identify what can move and what should stay.

  • Plan the sequence

    First fix and pressure testing happen before pipework is covered. Electrical, tiling and waterproofing work remain separate and must be scheduled around that plumbing sequence.

  • Install and test the first fix

    Supply and waste runs are installed and tested while every joint is still accessible.

  • Return for fittings and handover

    When the surrounding surfaces are ready, I fit and connect the agreed bathroom items, test supply and drainage under use, and hand over my work.

    Does full renovation mean you do the electrical and tiling work?

    No. My direct scope is plumbing and directly related fitting work. Electrical work, tile installation and waterproofing are separate specialist trades.

    Can you help me find an electrician or tiler?

    You can ask and I may point you to someone reliable, but that is an informal referral rather than a service I sell or manage. Their quote and agreement are separate.

    When should the plumbing be tested?

    Before it is covered. First-fix supply and waste pipework should be tested while joints remain accessible, then fittings are tested again at second fix.

    Can you move the WC or basin?

    Sometimes. It depends on the soil stack, waste route, available fall and structure. I confirm what is practical before the layout is treated as final.
