I repair and regrout grout on existing bathroom tiles where joints have cracked, worn away, stained beyond cleaning or become patchy.

The work is limited to existing tiled surfaces. It does not include new tile installation, tile replacement, waterproofing, demolition or rebuilding the surface behind loose tiles.

Grout is a finish between tiles, not a substitute for a waterproofing system. If the real problem is a plumbing leak, loose tile bed or failed waterproofing, I will say so instead of selling regrouting as a cure.

  • Check that regrouting is appropriate
