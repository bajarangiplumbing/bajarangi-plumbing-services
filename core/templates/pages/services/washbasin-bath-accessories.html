This service combines washbasin plumbing with the connected and non-plumbing accessories that complete a bathroom.

I fit pedestal, counter-top and wall-hung basins; taps, mixers, showers, health faucets and angle valves; and accessories including towel rails, mirrors, shelves and grab bars. Before drilling, I check the fixing location and the surface so each item gets a suitable fixing rather than a screw chosen by habit.

Electrical accessories and electrical connections are not included.

  • Check positions and surfaces

    I confirm heights, clearances, pipe points and the wall or counter construction before drilling or connecting anything.

  • Use the right fixing

    A basin, grab bar and lightweight shelf do not take the same load. Each gets a fixing suited to the item and surface.

  • Connect and seal plumbing

    Supply and waste connections are assembled without strain, sealed correctly and left accessible where they should be.

  • Align and test everything

    Accessories are level and secure; plumbing fittings are run under normal pressure and checked for leaks and drainage.
