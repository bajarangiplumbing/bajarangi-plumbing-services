I fit and replace WCs and the plumbing that makes them work, from the inlet valve and cistern to the flush connection and soil outlet.

Before fitting anything, I check the outlet position, water connection and available space against the WC you have chosen. A model that does not match the existing outlet can turn a simple replacement into unnecessary alteration, so it is worth checking before you buy.

This service covers toilet fitting and its directly related plumbing. It does not include electrical work, tile installation or waterproofing.

    Can you replace my WC with any model?

    Not always as a direct swap. The outlet direction, distance from the wall and water point must suit the new WC. Send the model details before buying and I can check the basics.

    Do you fit concealed cisterns?

    Yes, for the plumbing and fitting work. Any wall construction or tile installation needed around it must be handled separately.

    Can you repair a running toilet instead of replacing it?

    Often. A fill valve, flush valve, seal or connection may be the only failed part. I will tell you when repair is sensible and when replacement is better value.
