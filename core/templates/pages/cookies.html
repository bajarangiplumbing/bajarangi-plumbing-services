THE CONCLUSION THIS PAGE DOCUMENTS: this site does not need a cookie consent banner, and adding one would be worse than not adding one. The reasoning, which was checked against the code rather than assumed: - The only cookies set are Django's `csrftoken` and `sessionid`. core/security.py and plumber_site/settings.py contain no set_cookie call, and a grep for `document.cookie` across static/js returns nothing. - `csrftoken` is a security control - it is what stops a third-party page submitting the lead form on a visitor's behalf. `sessionid` is only ever issued at /admin/ login, i.e. to the owner, never to a visitor. - There is no analytics, no Google Tag Manager, no advertising pixel, no embedded map, no embedded video, no web font fetched from a CDN, and no third-party script of any kind. base.html loads exactly one stylesheet and one script, both same-origin. So there is no non-essential storage to consent to. - The DPDP Act 2023 requires consent for processing personal data, which is what brings tracking cookies into scope. Strictly necessary functional cookies of this kind are not what that duty is aimed at, and there is nothing here to opt out of. Why not add a banner "to be safe": a consent dialogue that controls nothing trains people to dismiss consent dialogues, and it misrepresents the site as doing tracking it does not do. It is also the single most common cargo-culted compliance artefact, so its presence alongside no analytics would suggest the rest of the compliance work was copied rather than reasoned about. IF THAT EVER CHANGES: the moment any analytics, embedded map, embedded video, remote font or advertising tag is added, this page stops being accurate and a real consent mechanism becomes necessary - prior, opt-in, granular, and as easy to refuse as to accept. Cookie policy

Two cookies, both doing a necessary job, neither of them watching you. No analytics, no advertising, and no consent pop-up — because there is nothing here to consent to.

This page lists every cookie this website sets and explains why you have not been shown a cookie banner. Last updated {{ policy_updated }}.

Why there is no cookie pop-up

Cookie banners exist to get your permission before a site starts tracking you. This site does not track you, so there is nothing to ask permission for.

To be specific about what that means: there is no Google Analytics, no Google Tag Manager, no Facebook or Meta pixel, no advertising tag, no heatmap or session-recording tool, no embedded map, no embedded video, and no font or script loaded from someone else's server. The whole site is one stylesheet and one small script, both served from this domain.

I would rather tell you that plainly than show you a dialogue that pretends to give you a choice about something that is not happening.

The cookies that are set

Both are what the law calls strictly necessary: remove them and the site either stops being secure or stops working.

csrftoken

  • What it does: proves that a form submission came from this website and not from a different site acting in your name. It is a security measure, and it is the reason nobody can make your browser send me a plumbing enquiry without you knowing.
  • Set when: you load any page with a form on it.
  • Contains: a random token. No name, no number, nothing about you.
  • Lifetime: up to one year, or until you clear your browser data.
  • First or third party: first party. It never leaves this domain.

sessionid

  • What it does: keeps me logged in to the admin console where I read enquiries.
  • Set when: only when someone signs in at the staff login. As an ordinary visitor you will never receive this cookie.
  • Contains: a random session identifier.
  • Lifetime: two weeks, or until sign-out.
  • First or third party: first party.

Other storage on your device

None. An earlier version of this website kept a copy of what you typed into the form in your browser's local storage. That was removed, and the site now actively clears it from any device that still has it left over from a previous visit.

If you want to block them anyway

You can. Every browser lets you block or delete cookies for a specific site, usually under Settings, then Privacy. Blocking csrftoken will stop the enquiry forms working, because the server will not be able to tell a genuine submission from a forged one. Everything else on the site — every page, the phone number, the WhatsApp links — works fine without cookies.

If this ever changes

If I ever add anything that does track you — a visitor-counting tool, an embedded map, an advertising tag — then this page will be updated before it goes live and you will be asked for consent properly first, with a genuine option to refuse. I will not quietly start tracking and rely on a banner you clicked past.
