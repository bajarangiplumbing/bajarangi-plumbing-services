Questions people ask before they call

How soon can you come out?

It depends on where you are and what is already booked that day. I will not promise an arrival time before we have spoken, because a time I cannot keep is worse than no time at all.

Message me with your locality and the problem and I will tell you honestly when I can be there.

How does pricing work?

The ₹150 visit charge applies ONLY if the customer declines the repair after inspection — there is no charge if the customer proceeds with the repair. I look at the job, tell you what it needs and what it will cost, and you decide before I start. The quote is itemised, so you can see what is labour and what is parts.

GST is not currently applicable, because turnover is below the ₹20 lakh/year threshold requiring registration under Indian GST law.

If I open something up and find the job is bigger than it looked, I stop and tell you before carrying on. No surprises at the end.

Which parts of Bhubaneswar do you cover?

I cover all areas within Bhubaneswar city limits.

If you are just outside, ask anyway — it often depends on the day's schedule.

What should I do before you arrive?

Find your stop valve and close it if water is running. Move anything you care about away from the wet area. If it is a tank or motor problem, switch the motor off at the mains rather than leaving it running dry.

A photo or a short video sent on WhatsApp genuinely helps — it means I can often bring the right part the first time.

How do I book you?

Fill in the short form on this page. It opens WhatsApp with your name, number, locality and problem already written, and you tap Send. A website cannot send a WhatsApp message on your behalf, so that last tap is yours.

Or just call. That works too.
